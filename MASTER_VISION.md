# Master Vision

1220 Energy Solutions is the independent research vehicle for a longer-term conceptual framework called Gardeners of the Galaxy.

Within that framework:

- **The Forge** — Spherical magnetic geometry under study for charged-particle dynamics and potential neutron-source applications.
- **Operation Pink Eye** — Current computational campaign characterizing the magnetic topology and single-particle behavior of The Forge.
- **Aquarius One** — Conceptual buried immersion-cooled computational infrastructure (not demonstrated).
- **Ark Perplexity** — Conceptual ocean-restoration vessel concept (not demonstrated).
- **Project Sunshine** — Conceptual element within the broader framework (not demonstrated).

All of the above except the ongoing computational work on The Forge are conceptual designs. None have been built or demonstrated.

The present public repository documents only the computational investigation of charged-particle motion in an idealized spherical magnetic field.
