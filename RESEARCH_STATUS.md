# Research Status

Last updated: 2026-11-24T18:43:00Z

## Completed Experiments

**Experiment 5** (2026-11-23)  
Short-timescale (2 µs) Boris integration of a 10 eV proton.  
Observed position convergence order ≈ 2.000 between 20 ps, 10 ps, and 5 ps.  
Final radii agreed to < 0.001 mm. Speed drift < 1e-14.  
Source: private research branch, Experiment 5.

**Experiment 7** (in progress as of 2026-11-24)  
20 µs trajectory at 10 ps and 5 ps.  
10 ps and 5 ps trajectories agreed closely through first near-center passage, then diverged.  
Final position difference: 19.954 mm. Velocity difference: 10,140 m/s.  
Indicates need for finer timesteps before claiming long-duration convergence.

**Experiment 8 / 11** (completed)  
Radial outward launch (10 eV, 30 cm) reached ~38.75 cm in 2 µs and crossed the 40 cm diagnostic boundary at approximately 2.285 µs.  
Suggests a possible symmetry-axis loss channel.

**Experiment 10** (post-processing of Experiment 7)  
Pitch angle and local |B| sampled at 1 µs intervals.  
Field magnitude dropped by many orders of magnitude near center.  
Pitch angle reversed near closest approach; may reflect field-direction change rather than particle motion reversal.  
Closest approach not fully resolved (1 µs sampling).
