# Experiment 16 · Magnetic Field Acceleration

The Forge · 1220 Energy Solutions · Operation Pink Eye  
Status proposed. Planning only.

## Research question

Can an interpolated field with direct evaluation in unreliable regions reduce end-to-end Boris integration time by at least 3× on a representative workload while preserving the field and trajectory observables used by Operation Pink Eye? The acceleration under study is computational. The physical model remains independent test protons in a prescribed static magnetic field.

Use the 32-face, 320-coil model and the Experiment 14 Phase 3 half-drift, rotation, half-drift Boris step. Swap only the field evaluator at the force midpoint in matched tests. The reference is float64 Magpylib evaluation of the full coil sum, with the same uniform +z bias added analytically to every evaluator. Record results separately at 0, 0.01, 0.1 and 1 T. A common coil-field cache can serve all four biases, but reliability masks must be qualified on the **total** field for each bias.

## Validation context

Earlier direct-field runs motivate three stress tests. Long trajectories can diverge under timestep refinement, the outward radial control crosses the 40 cm diagnostic surface, and local searches found off-center candidate nulls under nonzero axial bias. The earlier field map extended to 30 cm. This benchmark includes the 30–40 cm shell and a collar beyond the diagnostic surface.

## Field evaluators and refinement

| Evaluator | Design |
| --- | --- |
| Direct | Scalar Magpylib through the existing force interface. Also benchmark batched direct calls on the same query arrays and a matched batched ensemble where supported. |
| Uniform hybrid | Trilinear interpolation of Cartesian field components on a float64 grid at 20, 10 and, within budget, 5 mm. Apply the same reliability gates as the adaptive method. |
| Adaptive hybrid | Start at 20 mm. Split flagged cells into eight children down to 10, 5 and 2.5 mm, sharing direct samples at identical nodes. Use trilinear interpolation within qualified leaves. Retain raw interpolation as an offline diagnostic. |

Start with a cube from −0.46 to +0.46 m along each axis and qualify cells intersecting the 0.45 m sphere. The collar covers the 40 cm surface and the archived radial control through 3 µs. Treat a full force-midpoint location outside the qualified volume as a direct query. Use direct evaluation for cells with nonfinite samples, unresolved errors at the finest level, failed grid interfaces, possible nulls, poorly resolved weak fields, and locations near modelled coil filaments where gradients or source behavior cannot be resolved. Never extrapolate. Log and stop on a nonfinite direct result.

During construction compare center, face and staggered interior probes against direct evaluations. Trigger refinement on excess vector error, strong gradient variation, weak sampled total field, or evidence that field components could vanish together. A corner sign test can flag cells for investigation but cannot clear them. Use direct samples in cell interiors and comparisons across levels, including where a coarse model misses weak fields between nodes. Check both sides of coarse–fine interfaces and interpolation-to-direct transitions against direct values. Use shared conforming face values where feasible, with a direct halo around interfaces that fail the error or jump gates. Grow the direct mask around weak regions until adjacent interpolated cells pass. Sample the runtime force input unmodified when checking fallback, without using the interpolant to decide the true field at a masked point.

Create separate construction and held-out probe sets with recorded seeds. Freeze configuration before held-out tests. Use volume-uniform off-grid probes, stratified radial shells including 30–40 and 40–45 cm, high-gradient cells, grid interfaces, symmetry-axis points, and force midpoints from direct trajectories. Include narrow tubes around the radial launch and nulls, and sample off the axis. Use at least 1,000 held-out points per bias in each primary stratum (volume, outer shells, interfaces and weak/axis regions), plus the targeted root and trajectory probes. Report stratum counts and the sampled support. After a failure-driven revision, use a fresh held-out set.

### Null diagnostics

Seed direct root searches from the origin, archived candidates, and independent low-field and interior probes across the qualified region. For each bias, bracket weak regions with direct probes, solve for candidate roots from multiple seeds, deduplicate by position and estimate the local Jacobian by direct finite differences at two spacings. Record residuals and conditioning. Include radii of 0.1, 0.5, 1 and 2 mm in several directions around each resolved root. Search raw interpolation for shifted or spurious roots and use direct evaluation to check their neighborhoods. Search representative cells where interpolation remains active, including interfaces. Record extended or poorly conditioned near-zero regions as direct-only. Root comparisons apply to the searched volume and seeds.

## Benchmark protocol

1. **Field qualification.** Compare each method with direct Magpylib on identical held-out locations. Record vector and component errors, magnitude error, direction error where defined, transverse error relative to the launch or local velocity, and percentile and maximum errors by stratum. Report raw errors and delivered hybrid errors, interpolation coverage, and fallback fraction by reason. Boundary jumps are measured using two-sided limits or the smallest stable probe offsets, after subtracting the direct field change across those offsets.
2. **Field timing.** Use identical seeded streams for each applicable evaluator at batch sizes 1, 12 and 1000, with scalar and batched direct baselines where appropriate. Benchmark contiguous trajectory queries and dispersed samples separately. Time construction, mask checks, lookup, fallback and array handling, cache serialization, reload and steady-state queries. Warm up each method, interleave measurement order, and collect at least ten paired timing repeats on fixed streams for performance claims. Fix the interval method before collecting timings. Use matched hardware, affinity and thread counts, record load, and report median, spread and a paired uncertainty interval. Defer timing if host contention prevents a fair comparison.
3. **Trajectory convergence.** Compare direct and candidate paths at 10, 5 and 2.5 ps using shared 1 ns checkpoints, common observation windows and a 40 cm event detector that finds directed sphere crossings along each step chord. Use direct timestep comparisons to quantify integration error, same-step direct versus hybrid comparisons for interpolation effects, and timestep comparisons within each hybrid mode to detect error floors. At fixed 5 ps, compare at least two spatial resolution limits as a separate mesh test. Extract minimum sampled field, sampled gyro-angle, energy drift, velocity, radius extrema, crossing order and times, fallback fraction along each orbit, and the first time a trajectory tolerance fails. Compare through the full stated horizon, including following a crossing when the reference does.
4. **Representative workloads.** In short matched pilots replay the radial and tangential 10 eV launches from 30 cm, a 100 eV case from Experiment 8, off-axis perturbations around the radial control, and short segments initialized along direct paths near resolved weak-field regions. Cover all four bias masks in the field benchmarks and the radial and tangential pilot streams. For full-window qualification include the 3 µs unbiased radial crossing and tangential control, one off-axis stress case, and a shifted-null encounter for each nonzero bias claimed. Include the Experiment 9 seeded 12-particle sample for ensemble performance and event agreement over its 0.2 µs window. Treat 20 µs as a later extension with its own direct convergence reference. Publish the exact case list and query weights before timing. Time matched end-to-end runs with equal outputs and stopping criteria.
5. **Memory and amortization.** Measure cache size on disk, peak resident memory in an isolated worker during build, reload and integration, steady-state RSS and incremental memory over the matched direct process. Include masks, adaptive indexing, vectorized Magpylib temporaries and shared or per-worker copies. Three float64 components alone occupy about 2.38 MiB for a 47³ node grid, 18.4 MiB at 93³, and 145 MiB at 185³. Use bounded direct batches during construction. Compute cold and warm break-even from measured times. For an already constructed cache, reload cost divided by the measured time saved per trajectory gives the break-even trajectory count, rounded up. For a fresh cache include build and any required serialization in the numerator. If the candidate saves no time per trajectory, there is no break-even.

## Proposed acceptance gates

Freeze these starting tolerances before the first approved run. Each workload, bias and horizon claimed must qualify on its own evidence.

| Measure | Pass criterion |
| --- | --- |
| Field fidelity | At every held-out probe where interpolation is used, vector error ≤ max(10 µT, 0.1% of direct field magnitude). Direction error ≤ 0.1° for direct magnitude ≥ 10 mT. Require absolute transverse error ≤ 10 µT in the radial control and off-axis probe tubes. Require the interface jump attributable to interpolation ≤ 10 µT. Record errors also in the 1–10 mT transition range. |
| Weak fields and nulls | All tested neighborhoods within 2 mm of resolved direct roots and probes with direct magnitude below 1 mT must use direct evaluation. Route unresolved root regions to direct as well. Verify fallback returns the direct reference within 1 nT absolute on the same saved points. For root matching use a 0.25 mm positional threshold and a direct residual ≤ 1 nT, with stable estimates across direct search seeds and Jacobian spacings where the root is well conditioned. Inspect raw shifts and spurious roots even in masked regions. Any observed unmatched or materially shifted root in an interpolation-enabled region fails qualification until masked or resolved and retested. |
| Trajectories | Direct 5 vs. 2.5 ps, same-step direct vs. hybrid at 5 and 2.5 ps, and hybrid 5 vs. 2.5 ps must each meet maximum position and radius-extrema differences ≤ 0.10 mm, maximum velocity difference ≤ 1% of launch speed, and minimum sampled field difference ≤ max(1 µT, 1% of the larger minimum). At 5 ps apply the same tolerances between the chosen mesh and its next finer spatial reference, or evaluate that reference with direct fallback in the cells that would require further refinement. Record whether temporal error decreases with timestep and whether spatial refinement hits an error floor. Require relative kinetic energy drift ≤ 1e−9 in each run. Energy is an integrator diagnostic. Require review and finer direct timestep evidence for sampled gyro-angle > 0.05 rad on the finest step. |
| Passage events | Require identical directed crossing sequences and censoring classifications, and paired first-outward and subsequent crossing times within 1 ns. Flag grazes or censored trajectories approaching within 0.10 mm of the surface, events within 1 ns of the horizon, and cases failing direct timestep convergence as unresolved until finer direct checks establish a reference. Report near misses and unresolved cases in the denominator. |
| Speed and coverage | On each workload for which acceleration is claimed, require median ≥ 3× warm end-to-end speedup against the fastest matched direct baseline, with the lower end of a 95% paired timing interval ≥ 2×. Require ≤ 20% direct fallback across that workload's force queries and report per-case rates. A mostly direct weak-field stress case can pass accuracy while remaining outside the acceleration claim. Report the actual cold break-even and require it to fit within the planned number of trajectories using the cache. |
| Memory | Proposed pilot limits are 256 MiB serialized cache and 512 MiB incremental peak RSS per worker over the direct baseline, including build. Check total concurrent worker memory against the approved host allocation. |
| Reproducibility | Rebuild from recorded inputs, verify cache identity for identical numerical inputs or verify declared tolerances across platforms, and repeat held-out classifications on a recorded second seed. Any failed or unresolved gate restricts use to the validated domain, bias, horizon and workload. Use direct evaluation for other scientific runs. |

## Approval, cost and records

Approval is required before each compute stage. Proposed pilot cap is one worker, 500,000 direct observer points across construction, field validation and field timing, 25,000 scalar-equivalent direct trajectory steps including timing repeats, and 30 minutes wall time, whichever comes first. Record batches and count each observer position once per direct call that evaluates it. Prioritize the 20 mm and first adaptive level before finer uniform grids. A complete 5 mm cube would need over 6 million grid nodes, so its pilot comparison is limited to matched subdomains. Report incomplete measurements if a cap is reached. Use fresh run IDs and preserve existing records and jobs.

A planning estimate of 6.79 ms per scalar step gives roughly 3 minutes for 25,000 trajectory steps and roughly 4 hours for **one** 3 µs case across 10, 5 and 2.5 ps (2.1 million direct steps). Field construction cost depends on batching and coverage and must be measured. Request a separate full-window budget after the pilot, with projected observer and step counts, wall time, memory, and stop conditions.

Save a run manifest with model, configuration, evaluator, integrator, input and cache hashes, geometry and units, biases, exact initial conditions, timesteps and horizons, grid levels and masks, probe seeds and splits, tolerances, software versions, hardware and timing configuration, and completion status. Write raw paired accuracy, null, timing, trajectory and memory summaries, including failures and coverage.
